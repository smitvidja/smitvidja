## 🔍 Design Lab — Deep Dives

<details>
<summary><b>⚖️ RegIQ — Last-Mile Regulatory Intelligence</b> &nbsp; <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/-Problem%20Defined-blue?style=flat-square"/></summary>
<br>

**The real bottleneck isn't finding regulatory information — it's converting fragmented sources into a decision you can defend.**

Indian regulatory data isn't a clean corpus. It's scattered across SEBI, RBI, MCA, and NCLT PDFs — inconsistently OCR'd, no machine-readable amendment graph. Answering *"what was SARFAESI Section 13(2) applicable to on 14 March 2019?"* means reconstructing legal state across time — a knowledge representation problem, not a search problem.

**Designed architecture:**

![](https://img.shields.io/badge/1-Corpus%20Construction-lightgrey?style=flat-square) ➜ ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/2-Temporal%20State%20Engine-lightgrey?style=flat-square) ➜ ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/3-Authority%20Graph-lightgrey?style=flat-square) ➜ ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/4-Source--Grounded%20RAG-lightgrey?style=flat-square)

**Wedge:** SARFAESI/DRT recovery research — smallest source surface, clearest buyer, real financial stakes per decision.

*Status: Problem definition v1.0 complete → feasibility research next.*

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🏢 WorkKin — Work Execution Platform</b> &nbsp; <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/-Blueprint%20v5-blue?style=flat-square"/></summary>
<br>

**Automation is great when the steps are known. Most real work isn't — it's "figure this out," then research, write, coordinate, and confirm it happened.** AI assistants stop at an answer. WorkKin doesn't stop until the outcome is verified.

**Designed architecture:**

![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Trigger-lightgrey?style=flat-square) ➜ ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Durable%20State-lightgrey?style=flat-square) ➜ ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Multi--Agent%20Execution-lightgrey?style=flat-square) ➜ ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Verified%20Outcome-lightgrey?style=flat-square)

- Durable workflow state (Temporal) — failures recover instead of restarting from zero
- 2D visual workplace — live projection of work state, not a UI flourish
- Behavioral identity layer, separate from agent permissions

**V1 scope:** Revenue & Lead Operations + Exception → Resolution

*Status: Full product/business/technical blueprint (v5) complete → sequenced after HireWise.*

</details>
